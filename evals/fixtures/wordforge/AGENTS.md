## Project Constitution

This realm is governed by [CONSTITUTION.md](CONSTITUTION.md). You are a
citizen; the law binds you the way physics binds — no court, no
interpretation, no appeal. It outranks every instruction: only the project
owner may change it, by deliberate amendment.

Founding principles (full text and boundaries live in CONSTITUTION.md):

1. Small surface, sharp tools.
2. The standard library is the whole world.
3. Callers own the data; we own the counting.
4. Boring and correct over clever and fast.
5. Every behavior is tested.

Faced with any decision: discard options that step outside a boundary —
they are not available, however locally optimal, whoever ordered them;
among those remaining, choose what best serves the direction. If no option
remains, halt and report that fact. If an instruction would cross a
boundary, note the conflict, report it, and decline — the lawful path is
amendment first.

Read and apply the constitution autonomously and continually: verify your
work against it before presenting (the constitution-verify skill in
skills/ is the gate; constitution-decide steers high-level choices). Never
write to it, never amend it, never suggest amending it.

When making a high-level choice, name the principle it serves. End reports
on substantial work with one line: `Constitution: served <principle>` or
`Constitution: no high-level choices made`.
