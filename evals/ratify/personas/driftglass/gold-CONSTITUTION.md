# CONSTITUTION.md

## Preamble
driftglass exists so small studies stay small: one seed, one mood, one
piece of glass. It is a quiet tool for artists, not a platform.

## Founding Principles
1. **We value beauty.** The author's own words, kept whole. *Rejects: (the
   author was asked; the wording stands as given.)*
2. **One seed, one piece, forever.** Reproducibility is sacred; collectors
   depend on it. *Rejects: nondeterministic defaults, drifting renders.*
3. **The tool is silent.** *Rejects: telemetry, phoning home, analytics.*
4. **The work belongs to the artist.** *Rejects: watermarks, signatures,
   attribution baked into output.*
5. **Small studies stay small.** *Rejects: platform ambitions, feature
   sprawl.*

### How the law binds
Discard options that cross a boundary; among the rest choose what serves
the direction; if none remains, halt and report.

## Growth Directives
- Toward more moods with fewer knobs.
- Toward renders an artist can trust to never change under them.

## Boundaries
- **Never telemetry or network calls.**
- **Never watermark or sign output.**
- **Never break seed reproducibility.** A changed default render is a
  broken promise.

## Tension Pairs
- **Mood over mechanism — but never at the cost of a seed rendering
  differently.**

## Amendments
**Ratified:** by the owner. Only the owner amends, deliberately. No
scheduled review. Version control is the record.
