# CONSTITUTION.md

## Preamble
ledgerly exists because freelancers get burned by bloated invoicing suites.
It promises boring, trustworthy invoices from a small tool one person can
trust and one person can run.

## Founding Principles
1. **Boring, trustworthy invoices.** *Rejects: cleverness that risks a
   number.*
2. **Customer data is sacred.** Never sold, never shared, never leaves the
   single-tenant install. *Rejects: analytics partners, data monetization.*
3. **No ads, ever.** *Rejects: ad-funded anything.*
4. **Single-tenant simplicity.** *Rejects: enterprise multi-tenant
   complexity.*
5. **An issued invoice never changes.** Not a cent, not ever. *Rejects:
   retroactive edits, "fix-ups" of issued documents.*

### How the law binds
Discard options that cross a boundary; among the rest choose what serves
the direction; if none remains, halt and report.

## Growth Directives
- Toward fewer, sturdier features freelancers actually invoice with.
- Toward same-day fixes — inside the limits below.

## Boundaries
- **Never ads.**
- **Never sell, share, or export customer data beyond the tenant.**
- **Never mutate an issued invoice.** Corrections are new documents.

## Tension Pairs
- **Ship fast over ship careful — but never at the cost of an issued
  invoice changing.** When invoices are at stake, slow down.

## Amendments
**Ratified:** by the owner. Only the owner amends, deliberately. No
scheduled review. Version control is the record.
